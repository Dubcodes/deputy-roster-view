Help

Using your roster

Back to settings

Re-Deputy makes your Deputy roster easier to read, while Deputy remains the source for confirmed shifts.

Notifications

Open Re-Deputy using HTTPS, then go to Settings → Notifications, tap Enable notifications, and send a test.

Re-Deputy can remind you about upcoming work and let you know when your roster changes. In Settings, choose one-hour, night-before or two-days-before reminders, immediate changes, a weekly changes summary, and eligible open positions. Send a test after enabling them.

iPhone or iPad: add Re-Deputy to your Home Screen, open it from there, then enable Notifications in Settings and accept the prompt.

Android: enable Notifications in Re-Deputy Settings, accept the browser or system prompt, and allow notifications for the installed site or browser.

Your calendar

Your shifts
The calendar shows Deputy shifts that belong to you and work days an admin has published to you. Available shifts are shown separately, so they should not be mistaken for work you have already been rostered for.
Calendar or list
The first button in the top-right corner switches between the month calendar and the month list. Its icon changes to show the view you can switch to.
Globe
For ordinary crew and Admin accounts, opens the shared crew calendar. Contractor accounts stay personal-only and do not show this control.
Person icon
Appears while you are looking at the shared calendar. Tap it to return to your own roster.
Previous and next
Move backward or forward by one month. You can also swipe across the calendar on a phone.
Coloured shifts
Colours are based on the location, so the same track keeps the same colour within your chosen theme.
Holiday star
A star beside a date marks a New Zealand public holiday. Tap or focus it to read the holiday name. It is date information only and does not change roster hours or calculate holiday pay.

Opening a day

Day heading
Tap a shift to see the full day. The heading shows your location, vehicle when one is known, position, race type, and rostered hours.
Manually rostered
Some office work, travel, training, or specially organised race days may be added by an admin instead of coming from Deputy. These days still count in your calendar and timesheet. A person without a set role is shown as Attending, and Making own way is kept separate from an unassigned vehicle.
Your crew identity
Your app account is how you sign in; it is separate from a crew identity and a Deputy connection. Some management accounts have no Deputy login or crew identity at all. When verified Deputy evidence or an Admin explicitly links you, published work days reach the right calendar. Crew without an app login remain visible.
Race Day
Shows the useful times from the roster note, such as trucks, office or Clow Place, on track, first cross, and the first and last race. If a Thoroughbred note is missing race times, the app may fill those gaps from the saved official Love Racing programme. A small Admin override label means an admin has deliberately corrected that value.
Crew schedule
Shows the positions, people, and vehicles for that location. A muted TBC means Deputy has a position for the day but has not given us a name. On matching Travel days, the participant list combines the shared schedule with linked users whose authenticated personal roster confirms the same Travel allocation.
Changed
Means something has changed since an earlier sync. Open Change History to see the old and new details. Reading it does not clear the badge.
Time calculation
Open the start-and-finish row to see the separately resolved outbound and return routes, last race, clearance, and pack-up. If a return route is not configured, the app shows the partial maths instead of inventing a finish time.
Your notes
Notes saved at the bottom of a shift are private to your account. You can also record a changed last-race time or when the day actually finished.
Making my own way
For today or a future workday, you can show that you are travelling separately. This is a reversible Re-Deputy preference: it never changes Deputy, and switching it off restores the latest crew vehicle from the roster.
Admin corrections
An active admin correction applies to that exact date and racecourse for everyone. It takes priority without changing the original Deputy note. If it is disabled, the page falls back to your own correction, Deputy, Love Racing, or the usual calculated default.
Track map
Race days can have a course map at the bottom of the page. The app uses the best saved map for that location, including a clearer image supplied by an admin when one is available.

Planning and open shifts

Open
An outlined Open badge is not a confirmed shift. It may be a Deputy open shift, or a Re-Deputy open position added by an admin. A Re-Deputy position shows Apply when it is offered to your team and you are not already rostered.
Open position and TBC
Open position means crew can apply in Re-Deputy. TBC only means the position has not been filled; it is not being offered as an available job.
Applications
Apply sends a local request to the roster admin. You can withdraw it while it is pending. Applying, withdrawing, or being accepted does not change Deputy.
Love Racing
A faded gold planning entry is a public race date, not a roster. Love Racing only fills missing race count or first/last race times. Deliberate Admin values win first, then Deputy, then Love Racing; it never overwrites known roster information or removes a known race day.

Timesheets

The small clock on a Sunday opens the fortnight summary. Days you did not work stay compact, while worked days show their hours and location.

Tap a day to open its full page, or open Calculation to check the hours without leaving the timesheet. Manual office and training days show their start, finish, break, and total without race-day calculations. Any private note you saved for that day is shown there too.

Settings

Settings is intentionally kept compact. Most options stay collapsed until you open them, while roster sync status and the main sync controls remain easy to reach. Advanced Diagnostics is mainly for troubleshooting bad imports, missing Deputy data, or login problems.

Sync my roster
Checks Deputy for your latest roster. Syncs also run automatically, and accounts are staggered so everyone does not hit Deputy at once.
Theme
Choose the colours you prefer. Your choice follows your account across trusted devices.
Change PIN
Changes the PIN used for this roster viewer. It does not change your Deputy password.
Update Deputy login
Use this after changing your Deputy password, or if the email or password entered at signup was wrong.
Report an error
Tell an admin what looks wrong. The report includes recent redacted sync information so they have something useful to investigate.
iCal backup
An optional Deputy calendar feed that can add fallback details when a normal sync is missing something.
Logout icon
Removes trust from this device after you confirm. You will need your email and roster PIN to sign in again.

Good to know

  • Contractor accounts use the same personal calendar, day, timesheet, Settings, Help and notification screens, but cannot open shared crew or Admin views.
  • Contractors are external crew identities and do not need a Deputy employee ID.
  • Re-Deputy remembers up to {{ trusted_device_limit }} trusted devices per account. Signing in on another device removes trust from the least recently used device.
  • Opening a change badge never marks it as read or removes it.
  • The shared crew calendar does not show private notes, open shifts, or personal change badges.
  • If the shared crew list misses your position, your confirmed personal roster can fill that spot. If the two Deputy views disagree, the day shows both as a conflict instead of guessing.
  • A future shift missing from one complete sync is marked possibly missing first. It is not removed unless another complete sync also omits it, or Deputy explicitly cancels it.
  • The latest applicable roster note and information are shown normally. Older and raw evidence is retained for history and diagnostics without presenting every initial TBC fill as a meaningful change.
  • Deputy remains the source of truth for confirmed shifts and rostered hours.
